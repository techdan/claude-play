Loma Staircase Loop

A hillside loop from 1828 Loma Street that strings together five public stairways, the Riviera Park gardens, a circuit of the streets around Belmond El Encanto, and Orpet Park. It starts and ends at the same front door.

Detail map and stops

  1. 11828 Loma St

    Start & finish · mi 0.00

    The house sits on the uphill arm of Loma Street, just below the edge of Orpet Park. The walk starts on Loma St in front of it.

  2. 2Loma–Pedregosa stairs

    mi ${MI2}

    Easy to miss. Two short flights drop from the Loma St hairpin, across a driveway landing, to Grand Ave at E Pedregosa St.

  3. 3Sierra Street stairs

    mi ${MI3}

    Sierra St dead-ends below the Riviera. A stair and short path carry on up to Alameda Padre Serra.

  4. 4Riviera stairway

    mi ${MI4}

    A long, straight climb from Alameda Padre Serra in seven short runs, up to the Riviera Park campus.

  5. 5Riviera Park gardens

    mi ${MI5}

    The garden circle at the heart of the old teachers’ college campus, now offices beside the Riviera Theatre. It is private property: if signs close it, go from the top of the stairway straight to Alvarado Pl.

  6. 6Belmond El Encanto loop

    mi ${MI6}

    Circle the hotel on public streets: north on Alvarado Pl, east on Mission Ridge Rd, down San Carlos Rd, and back along Lasuen Rd. The hotel grounds are private.

  7. 7Orpet Park

    mi ${MI7}

    A hillside park on both sides of Alameda Padre Serra. Take the short steps down, cross at the mapped crossing, and follow the park path east and down to Loma St.

Turn by turn

  1. Start on Loma St in front of 1828 and walk downhill about 40 m to the hairpin bend.
  2. At the bend, take the stairs on your right. Two flights, with a driveway landing between them, bring you down to Grand Ave at E Pedregosa St. (about 110 m)
  3. Turn right on Grand Ave and walk northwest to Sierra St. (150 m)
  4. Turn right up Sierra St to its dead end, then climb the Sierra St stairs and short path to Alameda Padre Serra. (110 m)
  5. Turn right on Alameda Padre Serra. Where Lasuen Rd forks off to the left, bear onto it for a few steps; the Riviera stairway starts on your left. (180 m)
  6. Climb the stairway. At the top, go right along the lane, then left up the central walk and a short flight to the garden circle. (170 m)
  7. Come back down the central walk, turn left along the lane, and take the steps and path down to Alvarado Pl. (90 m)
  8. Turn left and walk north on Alvarado Pl, with El Encanto on your right, to Mission Ridge Rd. (130 m)
  9. Turn right on Mission Ridge Rd. (320 m) Turn right on San Carlos Rd, downhill. (210 m) Turn right on Lasuen Rd.
  10. Follow Lasuen Rd west past Moreno Rd and Alvarado Pl, almost back to the corner below the Riviera stairway. (440 m)
  11. Just before the corner, step onto the Orpet Park path on your left and take the short steps down. Cross Alameda Padre Serra at the mapped crossing.
  12. Follow the park path east alongside Alameda Padre Serra, then down beside Moreno Rd to Loma St. (310 m)
  13. Turn right on Loma St and follow it west and up around the bend, past the top of the stairs from step 2, back to 1828. (310 m)

Left off this loop

  • Santa Barbara County Courthouse. 1.2 miles each way by the shortest walking route, all downhill from the Riviera and back up again. That would more than double the walk, so it belongs on a separate outing.
  • Bonita Plaza. Already removed from the route.
  • Rocky Nook Park, Butterfly Beach, Tacos Pipeye. Part of the driving day, not this walk.
